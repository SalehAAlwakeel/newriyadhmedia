[ 06 / FAQ ]

Frequently asked questions

Why a three-month commitment?

Month one, we're calibrating your content. Month two, we have real signal on what's working. Month three, we're starting to find momentum. We align every commitment period to the time it actually takes to win.

For paid programmes, we ask for a minimum three-month commitment because we want to be real partners — not turn-it-on, turn-it-off contractors.

Prefer to lock in longer? Six- and twelve-month commitments are available at a discounted rate.

Will someone actually understand our business?

Before your strategist writes a word, they run a written intake survey and a 60–90 minute onboarding session with you. They scrape your website, gather your brand assets, and ask every question needed to understand what your brand would — and wouldn't — say.

Rich context in equals rich content out. That's the whole model.

Organic vs Paid Ads — what's the difference?

Organic covers everything that costs nothing to publish: social media, SEO blogs, email, and Google Business Profile. Most clients start here — a proving ground before spending on ads.

Paid is campaign management for Google Search, Meta, LinkedIn or any platform you can run ads on. The biggest difference: you have to pay to reach people. Add it when you're ready to pour fuel on what's already working.

Doesn't Google penalise AI content?

No. Google's own published guidelines are explicit: they surface content that's helpful, reliable, and written for people — regardless of whether AI was involved. They don't penalise it. Same policy at Meta and TikTok.

Not one of our clients has ever been flagged. We stay on top of every platform policy update because if anything changes, it affects our entire business too.

How do we know it's working?

The first thing you'll see is that you're showing up — consistently, across every channel where your customers spend time. Content going out every week whether you have bandwidth or not. For most clients, that's the thing they've never been able to maintain on their own.

From there, we plug into Google Analytics so you can see what's actually moving: traffic by channel, audience growth, engagement. For the bottom line — calls, form submits, new clients — we work backwards with you. If we drove twenty extra calls in a month at 5,000 SAR, that's a 250 SAR cost per lead. You decide if that math works.
